How It Began

Rooted in Coffee Obsession

We set out with a simple premise: great coffee shouldn't be pretentious, but it must be uncompromising in quality. From dialing in daily espresso grind profiles to hand-shaping buttery croissants at dawn, our team pours genuine care into every step of the process.

Our cafe was designed to feel like an extension of your living room—warm timber accents, ambient acoustics, natural lighting, and the rich, comforting fragrance of freshly ground beans.
